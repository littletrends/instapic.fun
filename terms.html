Instapic Terms

Simple terms for booth codes, digital content and Magic Mirror event hire.

Your booth code

After a successful online payment, Instapic issues a unique 6-digit code for the package selected. An unused paid booth code has no expiry date and can be entered at a participating Instapic photo booth or magic mirror operated by Little Trends.

Keep your code safe. Anyone with the code may be able to use the session or view its associated content. Save, screenshot or download it after purchase.

Sessions and digital content

Each code provides the prints and digital features shown for the selected package. Once a code has been used to begin a booth session, it cannot be used to start another paid session unless Little Trends agrees otherwise.

After completing a session, use the same code to retrieve available photos and bonus content. Guests should download and save their content within the retrieval period shown on the website. Instapic is not intended to be permanent cloud storage.

Problems and refunds

If payment succeeds but no code is issued, a valid code is not accepted, or the purchased service cannot be supplied, contact Little Trends through the booth operator or the contact channel used for your booking. We will check the transaction and provide an appropriate solution.

Change-of-mind refunds are not normally offered after a valid code has been issued. Nothing in these terms excludes or limits rights and remedies available under the Australian Consumer Law.

Fair use

Do not attempt to guess, copy, sell, interfere with or misuse another guest’s code, session or private-event content. Access may be restricted where necessary to protect guests or the service.

Event-hire deposit and bond

A $150 booking deposit secures the agreed event date and is applied toward the final hire price. Unattended Magic Mirror hire also requires a separate $500 refundable security bond. Unless a chargeable issue is identified, the bond is normally returned within two business days after the equipment has been collected and inspected.

Care, security and collection

The hirer must keep the equipment indoors or in another approved secure location, protected from weather, liquids and interference. Equipment must not be moved, opened, repaired, disconnected or modified without Little Trends’ permission. Reasonable power, internet access where required, and safe access for setup and collection must be maintained.

Collection must be available at the agreed time. A 30-minute grace period applies, followed by $75 per commenced hour where access is delayed. Ordinary late-collection, reattendance and additional-hire charges are capped at $500. This cap does not apply to loss, theft, damage, deliberate misuse or reasonable documented losses caused by interference with another confirmed booking.

If the equipment cannot be recovered and inspected before a card authorisation expires, Little Trends may capture the security bond. After the equipment is recovered and inspected, the captured bond will be reconciled and refunded less permitted reasonable documented charges. Where collection could not occur at the agreed time, a $150 reattendance and administration charge may apply; this amount includes ordinary payment-processing costs.

Loss, theft and damage

The hirer is responsible for the reasonable cost of cleaning, repairing or replacing equipment lost, stolen or damaged while under their care, excluding fair wear and tear or faults not caused by the hirer. The security bond is applied first. Reasonable documented costs above the bond may be invoiced separately.

Theft, deliberate damage or misuse may be reported to police and an insurer. Any applicable insurance recovery will be taken into account so the same loss is not recovered twice.

Remote support and equipment monitoring

Instapic equipment may use automated and AI-assisted monitoring to report operational information such as power, connectivity, software health, printer status, paper availability and error messages. Little Trends may use secure remote access to diagnose faults or restart the booth.

Monitoring helps us support the service but cannot guarantee that every fault, interruption, theft or instance of damage will be detected or prevented. It does not continuously record general guest activity or private conversations. Cameras and microphones operate only when a guest intentionally uses a disclosed photo, video or audio feature.
