Penny Fever Privacy

How Penny Fever handles guest progress, VIP accounts, wallets and checkout records.

Your information in Penny Fever

Your keys stay with you. We never ask for, see or hold your wallet keys, secret numbers or recovery phrase. Please never share them with anyone, including us.

The XRP Ledger is public. Wallet addresses and transactions on the XRP Ledger, including card purchases and transfers, are public and permanent. Nobody, including us, can edit or erase them.

Xaman. When you use wallet checkout, we ask Xaman to open the signing, payment or receiving request for you to approve. The request includes the relevant item, price where applicable and a checkout reference. Afterwards Xaman tells us whether the request was signed, the transaction ID and the wallet address that signed it. We never receive your keys.

Playing as a guest. If you play without signing in, Penny Fever uses a guest identifier and stores game records on our equipment to keep your purse, chapter progress and earned items consistent. Your browser also stores sign-in, progress and save-recovery data. Clearing your browser’s storage can remove local data and your access to a guest save.

VIP saves. If you sign in to the VIP Guest Area, your email, sign-in verification information and Penny Fever progress (such as pennies, tickets, your place in the alley and saved photo codes) are kept on our own equipment. We also keep your linked reward-wallet address and checkout, mint and reward receipts so purchases and collected rewards stay associated with the correct account.

Other records. We also keep, on our own equipment, a log of each card checkout request (time, card, price, guest reference and request ID), counts of how many of each card have been minted, and a daily visit count that uses a random visitor ID rather than your name or email.

How long. We keep these records only for as long as reasonably needed to run the game, support your cards and keep proper business records. You can ask us to delete your VIP profile and Penny Fever progress, but ledger records can’t be deleted by anyone.

Requests. For any Penny Fever privacy request, use Message us. We may need to confirm your identity first.
