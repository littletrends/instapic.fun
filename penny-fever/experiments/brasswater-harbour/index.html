MARINA’S CAPTAIN’S NOTES

Give it a little nudge.

  1. Hold in the water behind the boat, within about three boat-lengths. The spray pushes it away from you.
  2. Move the jet around the boat to steer. Let go between bursts to refill the water tank.
  3. Pick up all three floating letters. Any order is fine.
  4. Bring them to the golden berth at the top. Release the jet, then hold ⚓ Hold to moor until the ring completes.

With a keyboard, focus the water and use arrows / WASD. Hold Space to anchor. Bumps are harmless; you can always try again.

The second and third voyages add gates, a ferry and a current. The pale dotted shoreline marks the navigable water.
