CELESTE’S NOTEBOOK

A little glass, a little light.

  1. Tap a brass glass to rotate it one notch clockwise.
  2. For a closer look, hold and turn around its ring. The beam follows your movement; release to settle into a notch.
  3. Light all the hanging stars together and send the beam into the golden sky bell. The tiny stars printed on the floor are decoration.
  4. When the path is ready, press Send the comet and watch your constellation wake up.

You can also select a glass with its numbered button, then use ↶ / ↷. Undo and Celeste’s hint are free. There is no time limit.

Chapter three adds a solid moon: starlight cannot pass through it. The silver-blue line on each glass is its actual reflecting surface.
