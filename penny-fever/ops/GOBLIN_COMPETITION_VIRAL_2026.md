## Market truth (2026 casual)
- **Hybrid-casual wins spend:** snackable loop + meta progression + IAP (not ads-only hypercasual).
- Downloads ≠ money. Block Blast! = installs; Royal Match / Monopoly Go / Coin Master = wallets.
- People pay for: **progression depth, LiveOps urgency, vanity/status, beating other people** (even without combat — ranked dress-up still monetizes).
- Browser: instant play, no download, cosmetics shops, multiplayer presence (FPS.io wave is hot but NOT our lane — we are carnival alley).
