Terms for Penny Fever games and Aura’s Tradeshow digital collectables.

Games and digital collectables

Penny Fever is a carnival game by Little Trends. These terms cover Penny Fever and Aura’s Tradeshow.

Digital trading cards. Aura’s Tradeshow booth in the game offers limited-edition digital trading cards, issued as NFTs on the XRP Ledger and priced in XRP. The price is shown before you buy.

Buying. You review and approve every purchase yourself in the Xaman wallet app. Once the transaction is validated on the XRP Ledger it is final, and we can’t cancel or reverse it. Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

Just for fun. The cards are collectables to enjoy, not an investment or financial product. We make no promise about their value, future price or whether you’ll be able to resell them.

Limited editions. Each card belongs to an edition with a set maximum number of copies, and some cards have a limit per wallet. The booth shows the limits for each card. Once an edition is announced, we never mint extra copies of it.

Transfers. Your cards can be held, sent or sold on the XRP Ledger. A card may carry a resale transfer fee that is set when it’s minted and applied by the ledger when the card is sold.

Your wallet. You’re responsible for your own wallet, device, keys and recovery phrase. We can’t reverse ledger transactions, recover a lost wallet or bring back a card sent to the wrong address.

Changes. Penny Fever is still growing. We may change, pause or remove games, features, prices and unsold cards at any time.
