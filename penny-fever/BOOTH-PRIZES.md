# Penny Fever — what you win at each booth

Live working list from MotherPC paper-games/prizes.js and paper-games/catalogue.js.
Each alley stall has six chapters: chapter 1 awards the first item, chapter 6 the last.
A second win of the same keepsake does not duplicate it (except moon penny, rose penny, star token and crown token, which can stack).
Workshop-only rooms do not write the pocket.

---

## Aura’s ticket booth

- Everyday penny — spendable currency; buy more here
- Booth tickets (ticket roll) — one ticket enters a stall; cash one at Copper Falls or Pip’s tables for five pennies
- Admission ticket — taken at the door
- Night suitcase — after you hand Aura the admission ticket

## Collection reward

- Moonlight wardrobe — after you have all six Alley Ephemera (ticket stub, pressed heart, lucky match, whisper charm, mirror shard, showman ribbon)
