# Art review — 9 September 2026

All 76 finished sets / 304 individual views were visually inspected on dark and cream backgrounds in seven contact boards. Larger spot checks covered the Mystic Tent, Ferris Wheel, pale character clothing and the cleaned character sheets.

The review addressed parchment rectangles, poster captions, panel borders, ground-shadow remnants, missing cream clothing and white hair, and cuts through props or neighbouring views. Structures were separated using paths through the background where adjacent views overlap horizontally. Character background edits preserve complete doll silhouettes and opaque fabrics. Thin white grid lines added by some generated edits were removed before export.

Nell’s left and right profiles were checked visually and reordered to match the standard output names. Her original sheet and generated edit both arranged those profiles in the opposite order.

The original overview posters remain intact as design references. The assets here are isolated subjects from the latest turnaround designs; the original posters themselves have not been flattened into transparent scenery.

The 35 structure sets preserve their source illustrations, including small differences between directional views and the existing illustrated surfaces inside booths. The 41 character sets use reference-based background edits that also re-render illustration details. Fine lines, lettering and textures have the detail available in these illustrated sources. These are four directional sprites, not rigged characters or 3D models.

Automated checks are recorded in `VALIDATION.json`: real alpha, opaque subject pixels, clear padding, PNG dimensions, frame/atlas equality, original source hashes, prompts, records and gallery data. Gallery JavaScript was syntax-checked with Node. Browser/GPU playtesting was not performed under the project’s local no-headless-browser rule. Game integration and in-scene scale/camera review belong to the current Mother PC build.
